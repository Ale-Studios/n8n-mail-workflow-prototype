What you need to get started

These are the steps of the setup guide, in short.

  • n8n, always on. It is the program that runs the workflow. If it stops, emails wait.
  • A connected Gmail account. It needs a free Google Cloud access, set up once: allow 20 to 30 minutes.
  • An Anthropic API key. Pay as you go, with a monthly spending limit that you set yourself.
  • A Telegram bot and a Google Sheet. The first for alerts, the second for invoices. Both are free.
  • A filled-in Company Profile. Services, prices, hours, tone of voice and signature: this is where Claude takes its facts.

Good to know before you use it

  • It never sends email on its own. Every reply is a draft that you review.
  • Claude can be wrong. When its answer is unclear, the workflow tells you instead of ignoring the email.
  • Attachments are not read: an invoice that exists only as a PDF lands in the sheet with the status "needs_review".
  • The text of your emails is sent to Anthropic to be processed.

All emails, names and figures on this page are made up. This is a free prototype. The video shows how the workflow is designed to behave; real results depend on your own emails and your Company Profile.
